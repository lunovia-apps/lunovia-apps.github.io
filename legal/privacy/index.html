Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Lunovia respects the privacy of visitors to this website.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit the Lunovia website or use links and resources provided through it.

1. Information Collected by Lunovia

Lunovia does not currently operate user accounts, payment processing, mailing lists, first-party analytics, or advertising systems on this website.

Lunovia does not intentionally collect personal information from visitors simply for browsing the website.

If you voluntarily contact Lunovia through an external communication method, such as email or a linked support service, the information you provide may be used to:

  • respond to your inquiry;
  • investigate bug reports or technical issues;
  • provide support;
  • improve Lunovia products or documentation;
  • prevent abuse or misuse.

Information provided for these purposes will not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate reason or consent.

2. Hosting by GitHub Pages

The Lunovia website is hosted using GitHub Pages.

GitHub may automatically process technical information associated with visits to GitHub Pages websites, including information such as IP addresses, for security, operational, and related purposes.

Such processing is performed by GitHub under GitHub’s own privacy policies and terms.

Lunovia does not control GitHub’s independent processing of information.

3. External Links and Services

The website may contain links to third-party websites or services, including:

  • GitHub;
  • GitHub Releases;
  • software projects;
  • documentation;
  • external software required by Lunovia applications.

When you follow an external link, the privacy policy and terms of the destination service apply.

Lunovia is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

4. Software Downloads

Lunovia may provide links to downloadable desktop applications.

Unless otherwise stated for a particular product, downloading or using a Lunovia desktop application does not by itself create a Lunovia website account.

Individual applications may have their own privacy, license, or usage terms where appropriate.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Lunovia does not currently use first-party advertising cookies or first-party analytics cookies on this website.

The hosting provider or linked third-party services may use their own technical mechanisms according to their respective policies.

If Lunovia introduces analytics, advertising, authentication, or other services that materially change how visitor information is processed, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

6. Data Retention

Information voluntarily provided directly to Lunovia may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for purposes such as:

  • responding to inquiries;
  • resolving technical issues;
  • maintaining support records;
  • preventing abuse;
  • complying with applicable legal obligations.

7. Security

Lunovia takes reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection and exposure of personal information.

However, no Internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The Lunovia website is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

If Lunovia becomes aware that personal information has been submitted by a child in circumstances requiring removal or other action under applicable law, appropriate steps will be considered.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, its services, or applicable requirements change.

The current version will be published on the Lunovia website with its latest revision date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please use the contact information provided on the Lunovia website:

https://lunovia-apps.github.io/
